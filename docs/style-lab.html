Rule

Flat colour only. No purple-to-pink gradients and no soft glow on everything, because those two are the strongest "made by AI" signals in 2026. The depth comes from outlines, cut shapes and motion instead.

3 · Buttons

Three ways to press

None of these use the thick bottom shadow. Each effect moves only position, size and opacity, so cheap phones run them without lag. Tap each one.

Danfo sticker

Recommended

Flat colour cut out with a cream border, stuck on at a slight tilt like the stickers on a danfo window. A press straightens it and squeezes it flat, and sparks pop out.

Adire print

Cut corners like a fabric label, filled with an indigo-dye pattern. A press makes the pattern slide and twist under your finger.

Neon line

A crisp double outline like an arcade sign, with no blur. A press sends a flash of light across the button.

5 · Player names

Legends meet their land
