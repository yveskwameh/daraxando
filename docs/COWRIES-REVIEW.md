# Dara X&O: changes for legal review

15 October 2026. Prepared for the lawyer who reviewed Dara Cowries, so they can confirm their opinion still holds. Nothing here is live: the game is not published yet.

**Approved by the lawyer on 15 October 2026.** Afterwards Dara Cowries were renamed **Dara Seeds** (another games app uses cowries). Only the name and the icon changed, nothing about how they work. This page keeps the old name because it is the record of what was approved.

## What you approved

- **Dara Cowries are points.** Players stake them on matches against other players, and the winner takes the pot.
- **They can't buy, sell, send or cash out cowries.** Cowries can never be bought with money, sold, sent to another player, or exchanged for money, airtime or prizes.
- **Free top-ups instead of payments.** Everyone starts with 5,000, gets 500 a day, and a free top-up when they run low. Nothing is ever paid in.
- **Players are 13 or older.**

None of that has changed.

## What's new

Players can now also spend cowries on their **day streak** (the number of days in a row they've played). The design copies the Duolingo language app, which most players already know.

| Item | Cost | What it does | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Streak Freeze** | 200 cowries | If the player misses a day, the freeze is used automatically and the streak continues | Hold at most 2. One is also given free at 7, 30 and 100 days |
| **Restore** | 500 cowries | Brings back a streak that just ended | Only within 3 days of the last day played |
| **Streak stake** | 200 cowries | If the player keeps their streak for 7 more days, they get 400 back. If the streak ends, the 200 is lost | One at a time, always 200, no larger amounts. Needs a streak already going. Never offered after a loss |

**One real difference from what you reviewed:** in the streak stake, the 400 is paid by the game, not by another player. Everything else is a cowrie sink: cowries go out of circulation and nothing comes back.

The screens call it a "stake", never a "bet". Every cowrie movement is logged on the server, with these reasons: `freeze`, `repair`, `streak-stake`, `streak-win`.

## One more change: the age confirmation

There used to be a checkbox, "I'm 13 or older". Now new players see one screen with a **Play now** button and this sentence right under it: "By tapping Play now, you confirm you're 13 or older." Tapping the button is the confirmation. The server still refuses to create a player without it.

## Questions

1. Does letting players spend cowries on Streak Freezes and restores change your opinion?
2. The streak stake pays out from the game rather than from another player. Is that acceptable as long as cowries still can't be bought, sold, sent or cashed out?
3. Is the one-tap confirmation enough for players aged 13 to 17, given the Nigeria Data Protection Act's rules on children's data? If not, what would you need: a checkbox, or a parent's consent step?
