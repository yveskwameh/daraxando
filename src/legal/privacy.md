# Privacy notice

Last updated 21 October 2026.

> **The short version.** To run the game we keep your player (name, animal, state and progress), your games, and your email or Google account only if you save your progress. We never ask for your real name, phone number or exact location. We don't sell anything about you, show ads or track you around the internet. You can delete your player at any time in Settings.

## Who's responsible

Dara X&O is a project by Yves Kwameh. In this notice, "we" and "us" mean the Dara X&O project. Yves runs it, and he is the person responsible for your information under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Questions, requests and complaints: [hello@yveskwameh.com](mailto:hello@yveskwameh.com).

## What we keep, and why

### Your player

- A random player id, your player name, the animal, print and pieces you picked, and the state you play for.
- Your progress: Aura, title, rating, Dara Seeds, wins, losses and draws, streaks, quests, badges, leagues and friend streaks.
- When you made your player and when it last changed.
- Which version of the Terms of play you accepted, and when, as our record of the agreement.

We need these to run the game you asked to play. On the first screen we suggest a state, guessed from your network by our host, Cloudflare. We don't keep the network address. If you don't change the suggestion, it becomes your state, and you can change it any time in You.

### If you save your progress

- **With Google:** Google's id for your account and your email address. We ask Google for nothing else: not your name, photo or contacts.
- **With email:** your email address. Sign-in codes and the links in our emails are kept only as scrambled copies, and deleted after 2 days.
- **With a player code:** a scrambled copy of your code, to find your player when it's typed, and the code locked with a key only our server has, so we can show it to you again.
- **When your code is used:** the time, and the phone's scrambled id, so your other phones can tell you.

We use these only to sign you in on another phone. We never send you anything you didn't ask for, and nobody else sees your email.

### Your games

- Matches against other people: who played, the mode, the moves, the result, the score and any stake.
- A record of every change to your Dara Seeds and why (a stake, a pot, the daily seeds and so on).
- The days you finished a match, which we keep for 400 days.

We need these to run the game, settle stakes, show your record, and count how the game is doing (see "How the game is doing").

### Keeping the game fair and safe

- **A phone id.** When the app first opens, it makes a random number and keeps it on your phone. We keep only a scrambled copy with each player made, signed in or played on that phone, so one person can't farm Dara Seeds with several players. We never read anything from the phone itself: no phone number, IMEI, model or contacts.
- **Bans:** if a player is banned, the reason, when it starts and ends, and any request for a review, with our answer.
- **Our admin log:** every rename, ban, seed correction and review answer we make.
- **If you ask us to help with a lost code:** your answers, the asking phone's scrambled id, and our reply, so we can check it's really you.

We do this because we have a legitimate interest in keeping the game fair and safe for everyone.

### Notifications

Only if you turn them on: the address your phone's push service gives us, the keys that let only your phone read our messages, and which notifications you want. We use them for the 7 pm streak reminder and "friend joined your room". This is your choice, and you can turn them off any time in Settings.

### How the game is doing

We count things like how many people played each day, how many came back, and how long Quick match took. The daily counts don't name anyone. To count returns, we use the days each player played (see "Your games"). Once the game launches, we also count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't follow you to other sites. We do this because we have a legitimate interest in improving the game.

### Technical information

- Our host, Cloudflare, uses your network address to deliver the game and to limit how fast requests come in, so nobody can overload it. We don't store it.
- If the game breaks on your phone, it sends us the error and your browser's details (its name and version, which can include the phone model), so we can fix it. These, and the server's own error lines, are kept for 3 days.

## On your phone

The game keeps a few things in your browser's storage, all needed for it to work: your login, the phone id, your settings (sound, music, vibration, nudges, notifications), the friend room you're hosting, the players that have played on this phone until you remove them, when we last showed you a pop-up, and how many matches you've finished on this phone, so pop-ups come at the right moment. The game sets no cookies.

So you can play Baba Ayo without internet, it also keeps:

- a copy of your player and of Ranks as you last saw them;
- up to 10 one-use passes from us, which let games played offline count;
- the games you play without internet (the mode, the level, your moves and the time), until they're sent to us.

When you're back online, the game sends those games to us and removes them from your phone. We check every move before a game counts.

## What other players can see

- **Everyone,** on Ranks, in leagues and in the Quick match list: your player name, animal, print, state, rating and title.
- **Anyone with your room code:** your player name, animal and state.
- **The other player in a match:** the above, your moves, the messages you send them (the quick ones and the ones you type), and whether you're in the app. Messages go only to that player, for a few seconds, and we don't keep them.
- **Friends you add:** whether you played today, for your friend streak.

Nobody can see your email, your phone id or your seeds history.

## Who else handles your information

We use a few services to run the game. Each only handles what it needs, under its own data protection terms.

- **Cloudflare** hosts the game, its database and its logs.
- **Google** signs you in, only if you choose Google.
- **Resend** sends sign-in code emails, only if you ask for one. It keeps a copy for 30 days.
- **Your phone's push service** (Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft) delivers notifications, only if you turn them on. Messages are encrypted, so it can't read them.

We never sell or rent your information, and we don't use advertising or tracking services. We would only share it if a Nigerian law or court required it.

## Outside Nigeria

These services run outside Nigeria. Our database is stored in Western Europe, and Resend and parts of Cloudflare, Google, Apple, Mozilla and Microsoft are in the United States. We chose services that protect personal data under strict laws and contracts, and we only send them what's needed to run the game.

## How long we keep it

- **Your player, progress, sign-in details, seeds history and ban reviews:** until you delete your player.
- **Days you played:** 400 days.
- **Phones your player was played on:** the phone it was made on, until you delete your player. Other phones, 400 days after they were last used.
- **Sign-in codes:** 2 days. **Google sign-in steps in progress:** 5 minutes.
- **Requests for help with a lost code:** 30 days after we answer.
- **Notification details:** until you turn notifications off, remove the app, or delete your player.
- **Error reports and server error lines:** 3 days. **Database backups:** 7 days.
- **On your phone, for playing without internet:** games until they're sent to us, and passes until they're used or 7 days have passed.
- **Passes for playing without internet, on our side:** which passes we gave your player, how the games played with each one ended (won, lost or drawn), and when it was used, for 10 days, so each offline game counts only once.
- **Matches against other people:** they stay in your opponents' history. After you delete your player, they're tied to an id that no longer leads to anyone.
- **Our admin log:** kept after a player is deleted, so a banned person can't simply start again. It holds the player id, what we did and why.

## Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you can:

- **see** what we keep about you, and get a copy in a common format;
- **correct** it (you can change your name and state yourself in You);
- **delete** your player and everything with it: You, then Settings, then **Delete my account**;
- **object** to or ask us to **limit** how we use it;
- **withdraw** your consent at any time, for example by turning notifications off;
- **complain** to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission at [ndpc.gov.ng](https://ndpc.gov.ng).

For anything you can't do in the app, write to [hello@yveskwameh.com](mailto:hello@yveskwameh.com) from the email you saved your player with, or tell us your player name. We'll reply within 30 days.

## Children

You must be 13 or older to play. If you're under 18, you need your parent or guardian's permission. If we learn that a player is under 13, we delete the player. Parents and guardians can write to us about their child's player at any time.

## Decisions made automatically

The game makes some decisions by itself: it refuses rude names, sends both stakes back when its rules against farming seeds apply, and pairs players by rating and state. None of them has a legal effect on you. If you think one was wrong, write to us and a person will look at it.

## Keeping it safe

- Everything travels encrypted (HTTPS).
- Logins are signed with a secret key, and sign-in codes and phone ids are stored scrambled.
- Limits stop people guessing codes or flooding the game.
- Only Yves can see the admin tools, behind a long secret key.

If your information is ever exposed, we'll tell the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours, and tell you if it puts you at real risk.

## Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your information, we'll update this notice, show it in the app, and say what changed.
