## Your player

- The first time you play, the game makes a player for you on your phone. You can change its name, animal and state at any time.
- To keep your player on another phone, save your progress with Google or your email. If you don't, clearing the app's data or losing your phone loses your player.

- One person per player. Don't sell, buy, swap or share players.
- You can delete your player at any time: You, then Settings, then **Delete my account**. It can't be undone.

## Dara Seeds and Aura

- **Dara Seeds** are points you stake on matches. The winner takes the pot. **Aura** is the progress you earn by playing, and it sets your title.
- Seeds and Aura are for fun inside the game only. They are not money, have no money value, and aren't your property.
- You can't buy seeds with money, sell them, send them to another player, or swap them for money, airtime, data, prizes or anything else, in or outside the game. Offers to do any of this, from anyone, aren't allowed.
- Everyone gets free seeds: a starting amount, a daily amount and a free top-up when they run low.
- We can correct seeds to fix a mistake, such as a stake lost to a fault on our side.
- To stop seeds being moved between players, both stakes go back when the same phone made both players, when one player keeps winning far more from the same opponent in a day, or when a player quits the first game before playing properly. The win still counts.
- Seeds, Aura and titles end when your player is deleted or the game closes, and nothing is owed for them.
